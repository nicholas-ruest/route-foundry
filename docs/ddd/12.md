## Language and ownership

## Aggregate and invariants
The aggregate records the decision-relevant input, current state, and correlation identity. Invariants: identifiers are safe; numeric bounds are finite and nonnegative; policy denial cannot create provider or durable decision effects; successful transitions carry a content-addressed receipt.

## Commands, events, and failures
Commands enter through the application service and emit context-specific accepted, denied, failed, or persisted events. Dependency ambiguity is an explicit failure, never an empty success. Recovery repeats a command only with the same idempotency identity or a corrected workload.
